# Series 7 lesson plan (covers every FINRA outline subsection)

- S7-1 (F1) Seeks Business for the Broker-Dealer from Customers and Potential Customers: 7%, 9 items
- S7-2 (F2) Opens Accounts After Obtaining and Evaluating Customers' Financial Profile and Investment Objectives: 9%, 11 items

Note: in FINRA's outline, margin sits in **4.4 (S7-4)** and options in **3.2 (S7-3)**. Margin questions use section S7-4.
