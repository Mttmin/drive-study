# Using Drive Study on an iPhone

Drive Study reads SIE and Series 7 practice out loud so you can study in the car. It is a website you can put on your home screen. The questions are original practice, not official FINRA items. A few figures change every year, and those explanations say to verify the current year. Lessons, quiz, rapid fire, a timed or untimed mock, mistakes, stats, and the 20-minute Drive button are all on the home screen.

## Install

1. On your iPhone, open Safari. The install button lives in Safari.
2. Go to https://mttmin.github.io/drive-study/
3. Tap the Share button.
4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
5. Open Drive Study from the new icon.

The first open downloads the app and the question files. After that, the screens open without a signal. The phone's own voice also works without a signal.

## A twenty minute drive

1. Open the app before you roll, while you can still look at the phone.
2. Tap Drive, 20 minutes.
3. It reads a short lesson piece, then mixes questions you have missed with new ones. SIE comes first if you are studying both exams.
4. After the question, a short tone marks the answer window. You get about 10 seconds in listen-only mode and about 12 if you are answering out loud. Say "wait" or "more time", or tap the countdown, to add 10 seconds.
5. In spoken mode, say A, B, C, or D. You can also say alpha, bravo, charlie, or delta. For a true or false item, say true or false.
6. A correct answer gets one line, not the whole explanation. Wrong answers still get the full one. Say "next" or "skip", tap Next, or press the car's next-track button to cut an explanation off.
5. You can say repeat, skip, explain, I don't know, pause, or stop.
6. The big buttons do the same thing. Use them when the car is noisy.
7. Turn on I guessed if you were not sure. A guess comes back later even if you were right.

Next on the steering wheel or headset skips. Previous repeats the question.

## The three voices

Open Settings before a long drive.

- Downloaded natural audio is saved on the phone. Each section has its own download and delete button, and the screen shows how much space it uses. Nothing is published to download until the audio files are added.
- Streamed natural audio plays from the site while you are online and is not kept.
- Phone voice is the iPhone's own voice. Pick a voice and leave it. It works offline.

If a clip is missing, the app does not stop. It tries a saved clip, then a stream, then the phone voice. The speed control, from 0.8x to 2x, applies to all three. The same speed buttons sit on the question screen.

## The other modes

- Lessons are short narrated sections with a check question.
- Quiz is 10, 20, or 30 questions, by section or weighted across the exam.
- Rapid fire is a term, then the definition. Tap Know it or Don't know.
- Mock exam follows the FINRA weights. Timed mode has a clock. Car mode does not. With the sample pack the mock uses every sample question and says so.
- Mistakes holds anything you missed or marked as a guess. It graduates after you get it right on three different days.
- Stats shows accuracy by section, weak topics, your streak, and a readiness number for each exam.

## Keep your progress

The first time you open the app, it asks for a name. There is no password. Everything you finish is saved under that name on that screen.

Safari and the Home Screen icon do not share progress. Settings, or the name at the top, can show a sync code. Copy it and paste it into the other one with Import. You can also download a JSON file and import that. Import replaces that profile on the phone you paste it into.

The app asks the phone to keep this data. Safari can still erase it after about 7 days if you do not open the site. A sync code is the backup.

## Locked screen

The phone voice usually stops when the screen locks. Downloaded or streamed clips keep playing more often, as long as you started them with a tap. The reliable way to listen with the phone in your pocket is the podcast feed. In Settings, tap Subscribe in Apple Podcasts. Or add the show from this address:

https://mttmin.github.io/drive-study/podcast/feed.xml

Each lesson is an episode. Each section quiz reads the question, waits six seconds, then gives the answer.

Saying an answer out loud needs a network connection on an iPhone, and you have to allow the microphone. The buttons do not need a network.
